# Sendas de Gracia Design System

Purpose: guide AI design tools and coding agents when producing prototypes, sections, or visual alternatives for the Sendas de Gracia website.

## Principle

Biblical truth at the center, grace as the path, and the local church visible as a worshiping and missional community.

The site should feel simple, reverent, clear, warm, and pastoral. Avoid a commercial SaaS tone, loud motion, generic stock aesthetics, or decorative excess.

## Color

Use this proportion as a default:

- 60% warm ivory and stone beige.
- 30% forest green.
- 10% liturgical gold.

Tokens:

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest green | `#254117` | Institutional color, navigation, footer, strong headings, deep bands |
| Liturgical gold | `#C2A46B` | CTAs, icons, thin rules, subtle emphasis |
| Warm ivory | `#F7F3EA` | Main background and visual breathing room |
| Stone beige | `#D8CFC2` | Secondary backgrounds, soft blocks, cards, separators |
| Graphite | `#444444` | Primary body text |

Rules:

- Gold must never dominate the page.
- Do not use small gold text on light backgrounds.
- Prefer graphite on ivory and ivory on forest green for readable contrast.
- Avoid unapproved bright greens, purple-heavy gradients, blue SaaS palettes, and saturated commercial color schemes.

## Typography

Headings:

- Prefer Cormorant Garamond for expressive, pastoral headings.
- Cinzel may be used sparingly for institutional labels, short headings, and references.
- Playfair Display is acceptable for alternatives, but do not mix all heading families in the same artifact.

Body:

- Prefer Inter for body copy, navigation, buttons, cards, and forms.
- Source Sans 3 or Lato are acceptable fallbacks.

Rules:

- Use calm hierarchy, not oversized display type everywhere.
- Avoid negative letter spacing.
- Uppercase labels should be small, tracked, and restrained.
- Body copy should be generous and legible, especially on mobile.

## Layout

Primary home structure:

1. Hero with mission, warm church imagery, and visit CTA.
2. Welcome section with pastoral clarity.
3. Meetings with Sunday service, Bible study, and location.
4. Pillars: Christ, Scripture, grace, and mission.
5. Sermons with recent messages and archive.
6. Ministries: children, youth, families, groups, and missions.
7. Plan your visit with what to expect, children, parking, and contact.
8. Footer with schedule, social links, contact, and navigation.

Layout rules:

- Use whitespace generously.
- Keep sections full-width or simply constrained; do not nest cards inside cards.
- Cards should be individual repeated items only, with subtle border and minimal shadow.
- Border radius should stay modest, generally 4px to 8px.
- Use thin gold dividers only when they clarify structure.
- Mobile should stack content predictably with visible CTAs and at least 44px tap targets.

## Components

Buttons:

- Primary: gold background, ivory text, subtle rectangular shape.
- Secondary: transparent background, forest-green border and text on light backgrounds.
- On dark green backgrounds, secondary buttons may use ivory text and a gold border.

Navigation:

- Main links: Inicio, Nosotros, Predicaciones, Ministerios, Recursos, Visítanos.
- Primary CTA: Planifica tu visita.
- Secondary CTA: Ver predicaciones.
- Donar may appear in navigation but should not compete visually with visiting.

Cards:

- Use ivory or beige surfaces.
- Use a fine border and minimal shadow.
- Keep icons line-based, simple, and green or gold.

## Imagery

Preferred imagery:

- Real congregation gathered under the preaching of the Word.
- Bible or pulpit visible.
- People listening, praying, serving, and gathering.
- Warm light, low saturation, reverent atmosphere.

Avoid:

- Generic landscapes as the main image.
- Corporate stock photos.
- Dark, blurred, overly cropped hero images when the church context needs to be understood.
- Artificially dramatic or noisy effects.

## Voice

Tone:

- Pastoral, clear, reverent, welcoming.
- Spanish-first.
- Communicate Scripture, worship, community, and mission.

Copy should be concrete and useful. Avoid vague marketing language, hype, and self-congratulatory claims.

Preferred hero message:

- Title: `Impartimos fielmente la verdad bíblica`
- Subtitle: `Para que conozcan a Dios y le sirvan en adoración, ministerio y evangelismo.`

## Accessibility

- Maintain high contrast.
- Do not place long text over visually busy images without a strong overlay.
- Ensure mobile text does not overlap or overflow containers.
- Buttons and controls must be usable by touch.
- Decorative icons and images should be hidden from assistive tech when they add no content.

## Anti-Patterns

Do not produce:

- A flashy landing page that feels like a product launch.
- Decorative gradients, orbs, bokeh, or abstract AI backgrounds.
- Overuse of gold, shadows, animation, or heavy ornament.
- Church content buried beneath generic visual polish.
- Layouts that require explanation text to understand the interface.
